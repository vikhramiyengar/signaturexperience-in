Questions we get asked

Before you enquire.

How far ahead should we book?

Four weeks is comfortable — it leaves room for the site recce, licensing and a cue sheet everyone has signed off. We have turned work around inside a week, but only where the venue and permissions were already in place.

Can you fire indoors?

Yes. Cold sparks, CO₂, low fog, confetti, petals, bubbles and balloons are all indoor-safe subject to venue sign-off and smoke-detector isolation. Live aerial pyrotechnics and flame projectors are outdoor-only.

What happens if it rains, or the wind picks up?

Aerial work stands down above the wind limit on the risk assessment — we will cancel an effect on the night rather than fire it outside its envelope. Indoor effects are unaffected, and we always carry an indoor alternative on the sheet.

Do you work with our planner and DJ?

Always. The cue sheet is built with whoever is calling the show, and we run our stack off the same timecode as the audio wherever the setup allows.

Is it safe near guests, children and the couple?

Cold-spark fountains are designed for proximity — that is the whole point of them. Everything else sits behind a marked exclusion zone held by our crew for as long as it is live. We stop the show rather than fire into a zone someone has entered.

What do you need from the venue?

Power at the firing positions, access for load-in, and a named contact in the venue's technical or fire team. We supply the documentation they will ask for.
